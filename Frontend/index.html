Verification Matrix

15 Mathematical Constraints

Hard constraints guarantee zero scheduling conflicts. Soft constraints optimize institutional satisfaction and student ergonomics.

H1 · Teacher Clash
No Double Booking for Faculty
A teacher cannot be assigned to teach two different classes in the exact same day and time slot.
H2 · Class Clash
Classroom Omnipresence Guard
A student cohort or class section cannot be scheduled for two lectures simultaneously.
H3 · Room Clash
Room Occupancy Collision
A physical room or laboratory cannot host more than one session at any given period.
H4 · Subject Duplication
Single Subject Per Period
A single class cannot have multiple subject entries assigned within the same timetable period.
H5 · Qualification
Qualified Teacher Mapping
Instructors are only scheduled for subjects they are explicitly certified and mapped to instruct.
H6 · Room Capacity
Safe Occupancy Limits
The allocated room capacity must be equal to or greater than the class student strength.
HU · Availability
Teacher Leave & Unavailable Slots
Declared unavailable slots or administrative leaves are strictly respected with zero assignments.
S1 · Weekly Load
Syllabus Quota Distribution
Satisfies mandatory weekly lecture count per subject as prescribed by the academic curriculum.
S2 · Burnout Limit
Maximum Weekly Teacher Hours
Caps total teaching hours to prevent instructor fatigue and balance workload across departments.
S3 · Back-to-Back Limit
Consecutive Lecture Pacing
Restricts continuous lecturing hours without a break, improving student focus and lecture quality.
S4 · Subject Spreading
Daily Subject Diversity
Avoids repeating the exact same theory subject in a class schedule on the same calendar day.
S5 · Class Compactness
Eliminate Idle Student Gaps
Minimizes empty periods between classes so students do not have awkward idle waiting intervals.
S6 · Teacher Windows
Compact Faculty Schedules
Groups teacher lecture hours into tight blocks, freeing contiguous blocks for grading and research.
S7 · Room Appropriateness
Lab & Facility Type Alignment
Ensures practical lab sessions are explicitly assigned to designated laboratories, not standard lecture halls.
S8 · Time Preferences
Preferred Faculty Slots
Favors preferred morning or afternoon slots for senior educators where feasible.
